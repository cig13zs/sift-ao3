Sift for AO3

Lists stay in this browser. Turn on browser sync only if you want them on your other devices. No Sift account, no Sift server, no tracking.

Saved exclusions

Tags left out of every AO3 listing and search you open. Sift adds them to AO3's own filter, so tags with the same meaning are excluded too and each page stays full.

    Muted authors

    Works by these AO3 users collapse on every listing, whichever pseud they post under. If you log in to AO3, its own muting (in your AO3 preferences) works too.

      Kept AO3 filters

      Filters from AO3's own sidebar that Sift adds to every work listing and search, like your exclusions. To keep some, filter a listing with AO3's sidebar (for example complete works only, a language or a word count) and press "Keep on every listing" in the Sift bar.

        Saved ships

        The primary ship filter turns on by itself on these relationship tag pages. To add one, open the ship's works page and tick "Only works where this ship is listed first" in the Sift bar.

          On the page

          These run in the browser on the listing you are looking at, including bookmarks. They do not ask AO3 to refill the page, so a strict rule can leave a page short. Kept filters, above, are the ones AO3 applies for you.

          A percentage of hits that left a kudos. It is a browsing hint, not a score, and it is not sent anywhere.

          Only the works already on that page are reordered. Later pages stay separate, because AO3 does not offer this sort.

          Work ids stay on this device so listings can mark or hide them. Sift does not sync this list and does not record a work until you turn remembering on.

          Presets

          Save the exclusions, muted authors, ships, kept filters, and on-the-page rules you have now. Applying a preset replaces those lists. It does not delete opened works or saved-for-later.

            Saved for later

            A private list on this device, for works you are not ready to bookmark on AO3. Mark one with Later on a work or a listing.

              Behaviour

              Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+S toggles Sift on or off (customize at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

              When a listing opens without them (a typed address or an outside link), Sift reloads it once with your exclusions and kept filters. It never reloads the same page twice, and never after you use AO3's own filter form.

              Back up and move

              Uses the browser’s own sync, not a server Sift runs. Exclusions, muted authors, ships, kept filters, presets, and on-the-page rules are included. Opened works and saved-for-later stay on this device. Turning this off deletes the synced copy.

              Export saves your lists and settings as a JSON file. Import adds a backup to what you already have; it never removes anything. A backup does not turn sync on.

              Moving from AO3 Savior or AO3 Enhancements? Copy their tag and author lists into the boxes above. Quotes and brackets are ignored, and wildcard entries such as *mimic are skipped because AO3's filter needs exact tag names.
