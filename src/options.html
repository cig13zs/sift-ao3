Sift for AO3

Sift saves everything on this page in this browser only. It has no account, no server and no tracking.

Saved exclusions

Tags left out of every AO3 listing and search you open. Sift adds them to AO3's own filter, so tags with the same meaning are excluded too and each page stays full.

    Muted authors

    Works by these AO3 users collapse on every listing, whichever pseud they post under. If you log in to AO3, its own muting (in your AO3 preferences) works too.

      Kept AO3 filters

      Filters from AO3's own sidebar that Sift adds to every work listing and search, like your exclusions. To keep some, filter a listing with AO3's sidebar (for example complete works only, a language or a word count) and press "Keep on every listing" in the Sift bar.

        Saved ships

        The primary ship filter turns on by itself on these relationship tag pages. To add one, open the ship's works page and tick "Only works where this ship is listed first" in the Sift bar.

          Behaviour

          When a listing opens without them (a typed address or an outside link), Sift reloads it once with your exclusions and kept filters. It never reloads the same page twice, and never after you use AO3's own filter form.

          Back up and move

          Export saves your lists and settings as a JSON file. Import adds a backup to what you already have; it never removes anything.

          Moving from AO3 Savior or AO3 Enhancements? Copy their tag and author lists into the boxes above. Quotes and brackets are ignored, and wildcard entries such as *mimic are skipped because AO3's filter needs exact tag names.
